## Changed

Drafted a two-tier OTel Collector topology.

## Why

Node-local collection and cluster-level enrichment have different failure modes. Keeping them separate makes ownership easier to explain.

## Blind Spot

Signal ownership was unclear when telemetry crossed node and namespace boundaries.

## Next

Write the topology down as an ADR.
